How to buy $MTA

Four steps, a few minutes. Get set up before 19.11 so you walk into the bank as soon as it opens.

  1. Install Phantom

    Download the Phantom wallet (or Solflare) on your phone or as a browser extension. Write your secret phrase on paper and never share it.

  2. Get some SOL

    Buy SOL right inside Phantom or on an exchange, then send it to your wallet. Keep a little SOL for fees.

  3. Copy the official address

    On launch day, the $MTA contract address appears at the top of this page and on our X. Only copy it from those two places.

  4. Swap SOL → $MTA

    Paste the address on Jupiter, pick your amount and confirm. Welcome to the crew.

Watch out for fake tokens: anyone can create an "MTA". If the address doesn't come from this site or our X, it isn't us.
